# Cross World launch film

- `cross-world-intro.mp4`: **exactly 4.000 seconds**, 120 frames at 30 fps,
  720 × 1280, H.264, no audio, fast-start enabled.
- `cross-world-still.webp`: the stationary official lockup, used for reduced
  motion, blocked autoplay, and media-error fallback.

The film uses only **00:00–00:04 of the supplied reference**. The aircraft
silhouette, photographic fill, and ascent are retained from those frames.
The generic “TRAVEL” title is removed and replaced with the existing official
Cross World logo and wordmark, without stretching or re-typesetting them.

The lockup follows the measured title ascent and settles on the same centre
line. It uses the supplied dark-background identity: orange crescent
(`#F36324`), white globe, white strokes, and the original white wordmark.
There are no later montage shots, extra glow effects, loading bars, or audio.

Logo geometry comes from `src/brand/logo-paths.ts`; its palette is defined in
`src/brand/Logo.tsx`.
